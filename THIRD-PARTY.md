# Rights and source use

The proposed BSD-3-Clause licence in LICENSE covers only Second Derivation's own review code and text. It does not grant rights to third-party papers, datasets, software or quoted material. This is a reversible candidate proposal for owner review, not publication or a new third-party rights grant.

The reviewed paper is [2407.16689v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.16689v2); its authors are W. Giarè, M. Najafi, S. Pan, E. Di Valentino, J. T. Firouzjaee. Paper/source archives and raw observational datasets are not distributed. Printed targets are attributed in the report and evidence trace. Saved outputs are generated review evidence, not redistributed third-party data. No external library code or wheels are included; referenced packages retain their own licences. Existing historical terms, where applicable, remain unchanged and are not superseded by this project-material proposal.
